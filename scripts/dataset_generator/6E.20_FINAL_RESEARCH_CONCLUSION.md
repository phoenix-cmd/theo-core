## 5. Stopping-Rule Argument — Why This Branch Is Being Closed

1. **The execution pipeline has now been repaired sufficiently to reach the actual scientific evaluation.** The eval-mode, autograd-contract, tokenizer, and provenance defects were all remediated; 6E.20.5 completed all 28 evaluations with Stage 7 integrity passing.
2. **The intended S4/S8 evaluation was genuinely performed** (condition-resolved, in 6E.20.5), producing meaningful behavioral measurements (H5) and geometric measurements (`cos(H1)`, `rho_c`).
3. **The experiment produced meaningful behavioral measurements** — a real, reproducible framing-sensitivity effect (H5 SUPPORTED; structured vs random readout separation in Cohen's *d*).
4. **The primary geometric observable subsequently failed its own pre-registered sensitivity validation** — `cos_H1(SENS) = 0.322966 < 0.95` — so its outputs cannot be interpreted as measurements of the premise.
5. **Therefore additional repetitions using the same observable are not expected to resolve the central question.** A repeat intervention would re-employ the same invalidated `cos(H1)` and can only reproduce uninterpretable numbers.
6. **Increasing training dose is not justified** by the frozen evidence: `cos(H1)` is flat across checkpoints (r = +0.07 vs step); the gap to 0.80 is ~0.34; margins are already large. "More training" is excluded by the data.
7. **Repeated recalibration of the same metric would create an open-ended measurement-rescue loop** and weaken scientific falsifiability. The correct response to a failed sensitivity control is a *different* measurement strategy, not iterative patching of the same one.
8. **A future investigation would need a materially different hypothesis, measurement strategy, or construct-validation framework** — not another pass through the current branch.

**Stopping argument (precise):** *The current branch is not capable of answering the remaining scientific question with sufficient measurement validity.* — NOT *"We proved the premise false."*
