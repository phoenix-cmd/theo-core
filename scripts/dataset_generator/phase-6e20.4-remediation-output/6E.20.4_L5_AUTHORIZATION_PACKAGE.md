## 4. Evidence for the Requested Change

The root cause was experimentally localized (Runtime Drift Root-Cause Localization Report, confirmed by reviewer) to an **execution-regime mismatch**:

- The historical anchor `0.428355` was generated with **LoRA dropout disabled in eval mode**.
- Stage 5 currently computes the anchor in **train mode with `LORA_DROPOUT=0.05` active**, injecting ~`1e-3` stochastic variance into `cos(H1)`.
- **Eval-mode reproduction produced `0.428355` exactly across three independent processes**, while train-mode evaluations varied beyond the `1e-4` gate.
- **GPU nondeterminism was ruled out** (eval mode is bit-identical on the same CUDA device).

Therefore: **this remediation restores the historical evaluation regime; it does not alter the anchor or relax the reproducibility criterion.** The `1e-4` gate remains exactly as specified — it simply becomes satisfiable because the evaluator now runs under the same regime the anchor was measured in.

