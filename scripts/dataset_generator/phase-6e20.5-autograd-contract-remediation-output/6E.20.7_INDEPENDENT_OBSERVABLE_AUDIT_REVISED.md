### Finding E — Four-cause classification — CLOSED
Spec Section 3b converts all four candidate explanations into explicit, pre-registered numeric criteria, implemented by Impl 2.4.
- **Common/background dominance:** `C_common >= median(C_common over conditions)` AND `r_cs < 0.20`, `agg` stable (~0.45).
- **Low condition-specific gradient signal:** `C_spec < FLOOR_Cspec_abs = 1e-3 * median(C_common over S4,S8)`, absolute negligibility; distinguished from (1) by absolute vs relative `C_spec`.
- **Per-sample heterogeneity:** `V_het > 0.25` OR multimodal `cos_H1_i` (>=30% in [+0.7,+1.0] AND >=30% in [-1.0,+0.1]).
- **Genuine stable plateau:** `agg` stable, `V_het < 0.10`, `C_spec >= FLOOR_Cspec_abs` at fixed angle, AND `cos_H1(SENS) >= 0.95`.
- **Denominators:** `r_cs = C_spec/(C_common + eps)`, `eps = 1e-9`; `FLOOR_Cspec_abs` scaled by run-time median but the *formula* is frozen beforehand.
- **Comparison windows:** within-condition (same c,t); across-condition (S4/S8 vs C4-Rand/R1-Rand at matched t); step-stability (`|agg range|` over {4,6,14,15} < 0.05).
- **Tie/overlap handling:** every criterion evaluated independently; all triggered flags reported with numeric values; primary = largest exceedance ratio; no criterion dropped; no post-hoc selection.
- **Pre-registration:** floors/windows fixed before 6E.20.7 data; Impl 2.4 reiterates floor formulas are fixed, only medians computed at run time.
**Verdict: PASS.**
