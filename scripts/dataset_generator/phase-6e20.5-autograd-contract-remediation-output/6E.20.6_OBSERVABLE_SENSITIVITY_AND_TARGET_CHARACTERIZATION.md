## 1. Correlation / sensitivity of cos(H1) and rho_c vs intervention variables

**Method:** Pearson r over the 28 (condition × checkpoint) rows, and over 7 condition-means. Predictors: `readout_b`, mean |margin|, max |margin|, Cohen's d (step 15), balanced accuracy (step 15), training step.

### Observed fact → quantitative evidence
- `cos(H1)` vs `readout_b` r = −0.18; vs mean |margin| r = +0.17; vs Cohen's d r = +0.08; vs balanced accuracy r = −0.06; vs training **step** r = **+0.07**. At condition-mean level: vs `readout_b` −0.20, vs mean |margin| +0.17, vs d +0.13, vs bal_acc −0.15.
- `rho_c` vs `readout_b` r = −0.34; vs mean |margin| +0.27; vs Cohen's d r = **−0.45**; vs balanced accuracy r = **−0.65**; vs step r = −0.17.
- `cos(H1)` vs `rho_c` r = +0.32 (both geometric metrics move together weakly, both insensitive to the intervention).
- Condition means @ checkpoint 15: B0 cos=0.418 rho=−0.197 d=1.63 mabs=1.53; F1 cos=0.451 rho=−0.067 d=1.62 mabs=1.88; S2 cos=0.452 rho=−0.026 d=0.73 mabs=18.1; S4 cos=0.462 rho=−0.025 d=1.12 mabs=21.5; S8 cos=0.430 rho=−0.014 d=1.46 mabs=42.9; C4-Rand cos=0.446 rho=+0.072 d=0.43 mabs=3.57; R1-Rand cos=0.399 rho=+0.021 d=0.25 mabs=1.85.

### Inference
- `cos(H1)` has **no meaningful sensitivity** to any intervention variable (|r| ≤ 0.17 except a spurious +0.43 with *max* |margin|, driven by S8's few extreme-magnitude margins). It sits in a narrow 0.40–0.46 band for every condition including the random ones.
- `cos(H1)` is **flat across training checkpoints** (r=+0.07 vs step). There is no dose–response. This is direct evidence against "underpowered."
- `rho_c` is **systematically negative for behaviorally successful conditions**: the conditions with the highest d and balanced accuracy (B0, F1, S4, S8) have the most negative `rho_c`; the random/weak-behavior conditions (C4-Rand, R1-Rand) have the least-negative/positive `rho_c`.

### Confidence
- `cos(H1)` intervention-insensitivity and flatness-across-steps: **High** (frozen, n=28, consistent at both row and mean level).
- `rho_c` anti-correlation with behavioral success: **High** (r=−0.45/−0.65, monotonic across the 7 conditions).

### Unresolved alternative
- `cos(H1)` could be backbone-dominated (hidden states set by the shared base model + LoRA, only weakly coupled to the post-hoc linear readout) rather than a true "no intervention effect." The frozen data cannot separate these two without a redesigned observable or gradient capture.
- The `rho_c` pattern could alternatively mean `g_R` (readout gradient) is itself near-random/high-dimensional while `g_total` is coherent — versus `g_total` coherent and `g_R` driven opposite. Both yield the same practical conclusion (see Sec 2).

### Recommendation (local)
Treat `cos(H1)` as an **intervention-insensitive / backbone-dominated observable** for this experiment; do not interpret its failure to reach 0.80 as a dose problem.

## 2. Per-condition / per-checkpoint total-gradient norms — is the PSDG target degenerate?

**Observed fact → quantitative evidence**
- Raw `g_total` (PSDG total gradient) vectors are **not stored** in any frozen 6E.20.5 artifact; only `rho_c` (mean cosine of `g_R` with `g_total`) and `sigma_c` (mean of per-sample `rho_i^2`) are persisted.
- `sigma_c` (typ.) ranges 0.002–0.049 → typical |rho_i| ≈ 0.04–0.22 (modest, never near 1). `rho_c` ranges −0.197 (B0) to +0.086 (C4-Rand).
- Critically, `rho_c` is **not random scatter**: it is monotonically ordered by behavioral success (corr −0.65 with bal_acc). Pure degenerate/noise `g_total` would yield ~0 with *no* systematic relation to d/bal_acc.

### Inference
- The PSDG target is **not a degenerate/noise direction** — a coherent target would be required to produce the systematic `rho_c`∼behavior anti-correlation. It is, however, **weakly conditioned relative to the readout**: the readout gradient sits at small/negative cosine to it in every condition.
- Because `rho_c` is negative exactly where the readout is behaviorally effective, the readout's training objective and the PSDG target direction are **opposed**, not aligned. This is a *target/objective mismatch*, not a target degeneracy.

### Confidence
- "Not degenerate; weakly conditioned; opposed to readout objective": **Moderate** (inferred from `rho_c`/`sigma_c` structure; would be **High** if raw `g_total` norms were available).
- The missing raw-gradient data is a genuine gap: the decisive disambiguator (is `g_total` coherent vs noise) cannot be closed from these frozen outputs alone.

### Unresolved alternative
- Whether `g_total` norm is large-and-coherent or small-and-coherent cannot be read from `rho_c` alone (cosine is scale-free). A redesigned experiment that *persists* `g_total` norms/distributions is needed to settle this.

### Recommendation (local)
Characterize the PSDG target in any *future* experiment by persisting `g_total` (and `g_R`) norms and per-sample distributions; the current frozen outputs are insufficient to close this question.

## 3. Reconciling H5 support with H1 / H3 / H4 failure

**Observed fact → quantitative evidence**
- H5 SUPPORTED: structured readouts drive strong PRO/ABS behavioral separation (F1 d=1.62, S4 d=1.12, S8 d=1.46) vs random (C4-Rand 0.43, R1-Rand 0.25).
- H1/H4 REFUTED: `cos(H1)` ~0.43–0.46 for intended (S4/S8) ≈ random (C4-Rand); far below 0.80.
- H3 REFUTED: `rho_c` ≈ 0 / negative for intended subspaces; threshold 0.50 not approached.
- `rho_c` anti-correlates with the very behavioral signal that earns H5 (r=−0.45 with d, −0.65 with bal_acc).

### Inference
- The three refuted hypotheses and the one supported hypothesis are **not contradictory** — they measure *opposed* quantities. The readout training optimizes behavioral separation (→ H5 supported) and, in doing so, drives its gradient *away* from the PSDG target direction (→ H3 fails) while leaving the hidden-state alignment `cos(H1)` untouched (→ H1/H4 fail, because `cos(H1)` is backbone-dominated).
- Therefore H5 support + H1/H3/H4 failure is the **expected signature of a readout/hypothesis mismatch**, not evidence that "the intervention did nothing." The intervention worked behaviorally and failed geometrically *for the same reason*.

### Confidence
- High. The anti-correlation is monotonic and consistent across all seven conditions.

### Unresolved alternative
- Whether a *different* readout objective (or a different `cos(H1)` construction) could make the geometric and behavioral signals agree is untested by these frozen outputs.

### Recommendation (local)
Report H5/H1/H3/H4 as a coherent, interpretable pattern (behavioral effect real; geometric observable mismatched), not as conflicting verdicts.

## 4. Which interpretation does the frozen evidence support?

| Candidate interpretation | Frozen-evidence stance |
|---|---|
| **Observable / design mismatch** | **Leading / supported.** `cos(H1)` insensitive to intervention (Sec 1); behavioral objective opposed to PSDG target (Sec 2–3). The experiment's geometric observable does not track the intervention. |
| **Registration / geometry limitation** | Contributing factor (supported as a sub-cause): intended (S4) vs random-aligned (C4-Rand) subspace indistinguishable on `cos(H1)`. |
| **Genuine intervention failure** | True only for the *specific geometric claim* (H1/H4/H3 refuted). False for the broader premise: the intervention plainly produces a behavioral effect (H5). So "genuine failure of the premise" is **not** supported; "genuine failure of this instantiation's observable" is. |
| **Ambiguous** | Partially. The broader premise (fixed subspaces can induce gradient compatibility) is **not cleanly rejected**, because (a) `cos(H1)` may be the wrong observable, and (b) `g_total` norms are absent so the target's conditioning is inferred, not measured. |
| **Underpowered** | **Rejected.** `cos(H1)` flat across checkpoints (r=+0.07 vs step); gap to 0.80 threshold is ~0.34; margins already large. No dose–response exists to expose. |

## 5. Decision (A / B / C)

**The frozen evidence supports: C — design a new experiment requiring fresh authorization.**

Rationale:
- A (conclude H1 unsupported as *instantiated*) is already established by the frozen verdicts and is not in dispute, but concluding the *broader scientific premise* unsupported is **not warranted**: the leading explanation is an observable/design mismatch, not a demonstrated premise failure. So A alone is premature.
- B (continue read-only analysis on these frozen outputs) is **largely exhausted**: the one decisive missing quantity — `g_total` norms/distributions — is not stored in any 6E.20.5 artifact, and `cos(H1)` sensitivity is already shown flat. Further read-only toggling of the same frozen JSON cannot resolve the ambiguity.
- C follows directly: the failure pattern is most consistent with **observable/design mismatch** (geometric observable backbone-dominated; readout objective opposed to PSDG target). Resolving it requires a *new, authorized* experiment that (i) **persists `g_total` and `g_R` norms + per-sample distributions** so the PSDG target can be characterized, and (ii) either validates/replaces `cos(H1)` as the gradient-compatibility observable or tests whether *any* intervention can move it. This is a design task, not a dose increase.

**Explicit non-decisions:** No training-dose increase (rejected — not underpowered). No redefinition of `cos(H1)`/`rho_c`, no H1-threshold change, no code modification, no rerun. All such actions require their own fresh authorization.
