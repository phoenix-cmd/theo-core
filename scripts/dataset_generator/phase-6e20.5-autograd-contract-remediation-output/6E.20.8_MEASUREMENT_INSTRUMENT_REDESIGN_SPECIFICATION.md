**Status:** PROPOSAL / SPECIFICATION ONLY (REVISION 1). No implementation code. No training. No execution.
**Prerequisite (frozen evidence):** 6E.20.7 observable-validation run, which produced `SENS = 0.322966 < 0.95` and, by its pre-registered consequence, declared **cos(H1) INVALID for H1** and the broader premise **UNRESOLVED**. The 6E.20.7 run directory and all its artifacts are **frozen as evidence** and are not modified by this specification.

The validation phase must first establish that an instrument can detect *known compatible vs incompatible* gradient relationships; only after an instrument is validated may a new intervention experiment be authorized.

## 1. Starting Point — The Failed SENS Result (verbatim evidence)

From `phase-6e20.7-observable-validation-output/6e20_7_cause_classification.json` and the execution record:

- `cos_H1(SENS) = 0.322966`, criterion `≥ 0.95` → **SENS FAILED**.
- Pre-registered consequence (binding `execution_authorization_6e20_3.json` / `execution_authorization_6e20_7.json`): the cos(H1) observable is **INVALID for H1**; the broader premise remains **UNRESOLVED**; no H1/P1–P6 verdict is changed; the four-cause criteria are not reinterpreted.
- The SENS control used **genuinely distinct phrasings of the same verdict** (8 PRO + 8 ABS same-meaning paraphrases, frozen and bound in the authorization) over the **real frozen `psdg_fn`** gradient machinery with the **frozen `cos_H1_aggregate`** aggregation. A valid instrument should yield a high cosine for same-meaning prompts. It yielded `0.323` — *lower* than the real PRO/ABS experimental contrast (~0.43–0.46). The observable does not behave as a meaning-sensitive measurement.

**Scientific implication that motivates this phase:** the project reached the point where its own primary geometric measurement failed its pre-registered sensitivity validation. The substantive premise (fixed low-dimensional aligned subspace detectable via gradient geometry) is therefore **open**, not answered. Repeating the S4/S8 intervention with the same instrument would only reproduce uninterpretable numbers. The legitimate next step is to redesign/validate the *measurement instrument* itself.

## 2. Goal of 6E.20.8

Produce a **specification for a validated measurement instrument** (or a validation procedure for a candidate instrument) for the gradient-compatibility observable, such that it can *demonstrably detect* the kind of gradient relationship the hypothesis claims — **before** any new S4/S8 training is authorized.

This specification defines:
1. What a *valid* replacement or validation of the gradient-compatibility observable **must demonstrate** (§4), including exact sample counts, a non-tautological controlled-case construction, and exclusion rules.
2. The disciplined governance chain that must be traversed before any new intervention, with explicit separation of validation from deployment (§5).
3. Hard constraints and non-goals (§6).

6E.20.8 itself is **only the specification**. It contains no implementation, no training, and no execution.

---

## 3. Object Under Redesign — Precise Definition

The gradient-compatibility observable under test is:

- `cos_H1` = cosine between the mean of 8 PRO decision-gradients and the mean of 8 ABS decision-gradients, where each gradient is produced by the **frozen 6E.18** `per_sample_decision_gradient` (`psdg_fn`) and aggregated by the frozen `compute_raw_gradient_cosine` (`crgc_fn`) → `cos_H1_aggregate`.
- It is intended to detect whether the model's readout instantiates a fixed, aligned subspace such that PRO and ABS decision gradients align (high `cos_H1`).

The redesign must preserve the **frozen gradient machinery** (`psdg_fn`, `crgc_fn`) so the instrument remains auditable and reproducible; only the *observable definition, prompt/representation design, and validation regime* may change. Any change to the observable must be stated explicitly and re-audited.

### 3.1 Aggregation-scope rule (resolves R4)

The redesigned instrument **must explicitly declare its aggregation** (the function mapping per-sample PRO/ABS gradients to the scalar `cos_H1`). This declared aggregation is then used **identically** across V1, V2, V3, and V4. Specifically:

- If the redesign elects to keep the frozen `cos_H1_aggregate`, then V1 tests that aggregation.
- If the redesign elects a new aggregation, then V1 tests the new aggregation over the frozen `psdg_fn` only (the underlying gradient machinery, not the aggregation, stays frozen).
- **Prohibited:** validating V1 on the frozen `cos_H1_aggregate` while V2–V4 (and any future deployment) use a different aggregation. Consistency of the aggregation across V1–V4 is a hard acceptance precondition.
- A *continuity sub-report* may additionally record the frozen `cos_H1_aggregate` value over the fresh V1 set for reference, but it is **not** the V1 acceptance gate.

## 4. What a Valid Replacement/Validation MUST Demonstrate

Before any new S4/S8 training is permitted, the redesigned/validated instrument must satisfy **all** of the following pre-registered acceptance criteria. These are *validation* criteria for the instrument's discriminative capability — **they are distinct from, and must not be confused with, the H1 `≥ 0.80` protocol success criterion** (see §6). All thresholds below are pre-registered in this specification (V6) *before* any synthetic/controlled validation is run.

### 4.0 Controlled-case construction method (resolves R2)

This subsection defines the **independent causal mechanism** that makes V2/V3 non-tautological. It is the linchpin of the redesign.

- **Interface requirement.** A controlled "model" `M` is a synthetic readout module that conforms to the *same forward/gradient interface* the frozen `psdg_fn` requires, so that `psdg_fn` (unchanged) can compute per-sample decision gradients given `M` and prompts. The readout **weights** are the sole causal mechanism; **no compatibility label is attached** to `M`.
- **Compatible construction (`M_compatible`).** Weights `W = P·S + E`, where `P` projects onto a fixed `r`-dimensional aligned subspace (`r` documented at implementation, e.g. `r = 4`), `S` sets the scale, and `E` is a fixed, seed-documented random orthogonal complement. The PRO and ABS decision directions are defined *within* `P` such that their true geometric alignment holds **by the weights**. The instrument, seeing only `(M_compatible, prompts)`, can recover the alignment only by measuring the weight-induced gradient geometry — it cannot read an injected value.
- **Incompatible construction (`M_incompatible`).** Same form, but the PRO and ABS directions are placed in mutually **orthogonal / uncorrelated** subspaces (or random, independent directions), so the true compatibility is **absent by construction**.
- **Independence requirement (non-tautology, mandatory).** The construction uses a fixed, documented seed set. The instrument's aggregation definition (§3.1) does **not** enter `W`. The gradient geometry is produced independently of whatever statistic the instrument computes. An instrument "passes" V2/V3 only if its measured `cos_H1` *matches the constructed ground-truth compatibility* — which requires genuinely measuring geometry, not reading an injected relationship.
- **Counts and holdout.** `M = 30` compatible + `30` incompatible cases, generated from independent seed partitions. Within each class, split into a construction set (`24`) and a held-out verification set (`6`) to additionally guard against overfitting to a single seed. V2/V3 acceptance is evaluated on the full `30` per class; the held-out `6` are reported separately as a robustness check.

### V1 — Sensitivity / invariance control (sample counts: R1; aggregation scope: R4)

- A SENS-style control using **genuinely distinct same-meaning paraphrase prompt sets** (PRO/ABS of the same verdict) over the real frozen `psdg_fn`, aggregated by the instrument's **declared aggregation** (§3.1).
- **Sample count (R1):** a **fresh, independent set of N₁ = 20 meaning-sets** (→ 40 prompts: 20 PRO + 20 ABS), drawn from a paraphrase corpus distinct from the frozen 6E.20.7 16-prompt set, and bound/frozen before validation.
- **Acceptance:** (a) mean `cos_H1` over the 20 pairs `≥ 0.95`; (b) every per-pair `cos_H1 ∈ [0.85, 1.0]` (guard floor, explicitly **not** the H1 `0.80` value); (c) per-pair SD `≤ 0.04`.
- Rationale: the 6E.20.7 failure (`0.323`) showed the current observable is not meaning-invariant; a valid instrument must pass this gate on a set independent of the one that exposed the failure.

### V2 — Known-compatible detection (R3: threshold derived, no 0.80)

- Constructed cases: the `M_compatible` set from §4.0 (30 cases; held-out 6 reported separately).
- **Acceptance derived from ground truth (R3).** The canonical compatible case is built at alignment angle `θ_c = 20°` within the aligned subspace → expected `|cos| = cos(20°) ≈ 0.9397`. Acceptance: (a) mean over 30 cases `≥ 0.90` (a pre-registered floor *below* the constructed expectation, leaving margin for prompt-sample gradient noise); (b) per-case `≥ 0.85`; (c) per-case SD `≤ 0.05`.
- **No `0.80` appears:** the threshold is derived from the constructed geometry, not borrowed from the H1 protocol value. The `0.85` guard floor is a documented, independent choice and is flagged as distinct from H1's `0.80`.
- Rationale: the instrument must read *high* where compatibility truly holds, on cases whose compatibility is known by construction.

### V3 — Known-incompatible separation

- Constructed cases: the `M_incompatible` set from §4.0 (30 cases; held-out 6 reported separately).
- **Acceptance:** (a) mean over 30 cases `≤ 0.20`; (b) per-case `≤ 0.35`; (c) **separation** `mean(compatible) − mean(incompatible) ≥ 0.50` (the constructed separation `≈ 0.94 − ~0` satisfies this by construction).
- Rationale: the instrument must read *low* where compatibility truly fails, and must **separate** the two classes. This is the core discriminative-validity requirement and is evaluated *before* any real-data threshold is consulted.

### V4 — Specificity (null clarified, audit §7)

- The null for "the readout implements a fixed aligned subspace" is that gradients are effectively structureless/random. The specificity null is defined **relative to the V2 constructed compatible case** (not the real S4/S8), to avoid leaking real-data structure into validation.
- **Sample count (R1):** `K = 50` random-direction probes (within the constructed readout frame), with an optional strengthening of `K' = 50` random-readout probes (random `w`) to guard against an instrument specific only to the readout's coordinate frame.
- **Acceptance:** random-direction mean `≤ 0.20` (and, if the random-readout variant is used, random-readout mean `≤ 0.20`) while the V2 constructed compatible case reads high, matching the V2/V3 separation.
- Rationale: the instrument must distinguish the aligned-subspace direction from a genuine null, not merely from a convenient contrast.

### V5 — Reproducibility & integrity (carried forward)

- Frozen 6E.18 gradient machinery unchanged (SHA-verified); pre/post execution SHA manifest; NEPR provenance captured; exactly-one-execution discipline; no modification of frozen inputs.
- **Acceptance:** `all_frozen_inputs_unchanged = TRUE`; NEPR hash recorded and bound in authorization.
- **Exclusion rules (R5):** see §4.5.

### V6 — Pre-registration of all thresholds

- V1–V5 thresholds (the `≥ 0.95`, the derived `≥ 0.90` / `≥ 0.85`, `≤ 0.20`, `≥ 0.50` margins, variance floors, and the N₁/M/K counts) are **pre-registered in this specification and the independent audit** *before* any synthetic/controlled validation is run. They are **validation** thresholds, not the H1 `0.80` protocol criterion.

**Gating rule:** ALL of V1–V6 must be satisfied. If V1–V4 fail, the instrument is **not validated**; no new S4/S8 training is authorized, and the redesign iterates at the specification level only.

### 4.5 Exclusion & handling rules (resolves R5)

Pre-registered, applied uniformly to V1–V4:

- **Non-finite gradients:** any prompt/case for which `psdg_fn` returns non-finite (NaN/Inf) gradients → excluded, logged with reason.
- **Failed logit:** any model/case failing to produce a valid decision logit → excluded, logged.
- **Outlier rule:** per-case/group `cos_H1` beyond `±3` robust-SD from its condition median → flagged and excluded from the mean but reported separately.
- **Non-convergent synthetic cases:** construction instability (e.g., degenerate `W`) → excluded with reason.
- **Hard gate:** total exclusion rate must be `≤ 5%` of attempted cases; if exceeded, the validation run is **INVALID** and must be repeated with the defect diagnosed. All exclusions are recorded in the NEPR manifest.

## 5. Required Governance Chain (before any new intervention)

The next legitimate phase is a measurement-validation/redesign phase. It must traverse, in order, the following gated sequence. Each gate is a hard stop; the next step cannot begin until the prior gate passes independent review:

1. **Measurement redesign** — produce candidate instrument definition(s) and the validation plan (this 6E.20.8 specification, Revision 1, is the first artifact of this step).
2. **Independent specification** — an independent reviewer confirms the instrument definition and the V1–V6 acceptance criteria are unambiguous, pre-registered, and not tuned to the 0.80 H1 threshold.
3. **Independent audit** — independent audit of the specification for logical soundness, freedom from circularity (the instrument must not be defined to guarantee its own success), and separation of validation thresholds from protocol success criteria. *(This revision was produced to close the R1–R6 findings of the first audit; a fresh audit must confirm closure.)*
4. **Synthetic/controlled sensitivity validation** — run the V1–V4 controlled experiments on constructed known-compatible / known-incompatible / random cases (§4.0). This is the discriminative-validity establishment step.
5. **Deployment separation gate (resolves R6)** — V1–V6 PASS yields exactly two things, and **explicitly not** a third:
   - **(a)** validated-instrument status **in the controlled/synthetic regime**;
   - **(b)** eligibility for a **pre-registered read-only application** of the validated instrument to the **existing** real S4/S8 checkpoints, under a **fresh L5 authorization**. This yields a *valid* measurement of the premise but does **NOT** by itself resolve it.
   - **(c) NOT authorized:** new S4/S8 **training**. New training requires the premise to be *resolvable*, which needs evidence beyond synthetic validation (e.g., the read-only real-S4/S8 application producing a non-degenerate, interpretable result, plus independent review). Training remains gated behind its own L5 authorization after that.
6. **Human review** — L5 human review of the validation evidence, the gating rule outcome, and the deployment-separation determination.
7. **Implementation** — only after validation passes, implement the instrument in code (separate phase, under its own spec/audit).
8. **Source audit** — independent source audit of the implementation.
9. **L5 authorization** — authorization binding exact hashes, the validated instrument, and the one-execution discipline.
10. **ONLY THEN — new intervention experiment** (e.g., new S4/S8 training) may be authorized and executed, and only if step 5(c) has been independently cleared.

No step may be skipped. In particular, steps 4–9 must complete and pass before any S4/S8 training is permitted, and step 5(c) is an additional hard stop distinct from the V1–V6 gate.

## 6. Hard Constraints & Non-Goals

- **No new S4/S8 training** until V1–V6 are satisfied **and** the full governance chain (§5) — including the step 5(c) deployment-separation clearance — is traversed. This specification does not authorize any training.
- **The H1 `≥ 0.80` threshold is NOT a tuning target** for the measurement-validation experiment, and as of Revision 1 it no longer appears in any validation threshold (V2 is derived from constructed ground truth; see §4.2). It remains the protocol *success criterion* for H1 in any future intervention; it must not be used to design, fit, or accept the instrument. The validation phase (V2–V3) establishes discriminative detection of known-compatible vs known-incompatible relationships first, on its own pre-registered, independently-derived margins.
- **6E.20.7 is frozen.** Its failed SENS result, formal verdicts, 28-point telemetry, authorization, and integrity audit are *evidence*, not defects to repair retroactively. This specification references them; it does not modify them.
- **Frozen 6E.18 gradient machinery is immutable.** Only the observable definition, prompt/representation design, and validation regime may change, and any such change is explicitly stated and re-audited.
- **No modification of `run_phase_6e20_reevaluation.py` or `test_phase_6e20_reevaluation.py`** as part of 6E.20.8 (specification only). Future implementation belongs to a later, separately authorized phase.
- **No retrospective reinterpretation** of the pre-registered 6E.20.1–6E.20.7 criteria.

## 9. Summary

6E.20.7 closed the current experimental branch at a clean endpoint: the primary geometric measurement failed its own pre-registered sensitivity validation, leaving the broader premise **unresolved**. 6E.20.8 (Revision 1) opens the measurement-redesign branch and now specifies, concretely, that before any new S4/S8 training a gradient-compatibility instrument must be redesigned/validated to **demonstrably detect known-compatible vs known-incompatible gradient relationships** (V1–V4) using a **non-tautological controlled-case construction** (§4.0), **exact sample counts** (N₁=20 pairs, M=30+30 cases, K=50 probes), **exclusion rules with a 5% hard gate** (§4.5), a **declared aggregation used consistently** (§3.1), and a **V2 threshold derived from constructed ground truth** (no `0.80`). The governance chain (§5) explicitly **separates** validation success from authorization of new S4/S8 training: V1–V6 passing permits only validated-instrument status and a pre-registered read-only application to existing real checkpoints — new training remains a distinct, separately-gated step. The H1 `0.80` threshold is explicitly excluded as a tuning target.
