## 0. Canonical definitions (read from frozen implementation)

- `cos(H1)` = `cos_H1_aggregate` = `compute_cosine_similarity(pos_mean, abs_mean)` where
  - `pos_mean` = mean over 8 PRO-framing PSDG total-gradient vectors `g_total`,
  - `abs_mean` = mean over 8 ABS-framing PSDG total-gradient vectors `g_total`,

- **Correction to a prior tentative reading:** `cos(H1)` is a *gradient* cosine (between PRO/ABS **total** gradients), NOT a hidden-state cosine. This is established by the frozen code and governs the analysis below.

## 1. Required questions (answered)

### Q1. What construct does H1 claim to measure?
**Observed evidence:** Protocol rule — `H1_Primary_Subspace_Gradient_Compatibility: mean_cos(S4) >= +0.80 AND mean_cos(S8) >= +0.80`.
**Construct claim:** Under the intended fixed, LDA-derived aligned subspaces (S4, S8), the model's *total decision gradients* computed for the PRO verdict framing and the ABS verdict framing become highly compatible (aligned) — cosine ≥ 0.80. The subspace intervention is claimed to produce a shared/aligned gradient structure across the two verdict framings.
**Measurement validity:** The claim is about *gradient compatibility* of the PRO/ABS total gradients.
**Unresolved uncertainty:** Whether 'subspace gradient compatibility' as a scientific concept is fully captured by the single aggregate PRO/ABS total-gradient cosine (vs. e.g. readout-vs-task gradient alignment, which is H3/rho_c). This is a construct-scoping question, not a measurement-error question.
**New experimental question:** Does the PRO/ABS total-gradient cosine fully represent the intended compatibility, or should the construct also reference the readout/task gradient pair?

### Q2. What does cos(H1) actually measure in the current implementation?
**Observed evidence:** `compute_live_cos_H1` to `crgc_fn(g_pos, g_abs)` to `cos_H1_aggregate` = cosine(`pos_mean`, `abs_mean`); `pos_mean`/`abs_mean` are means of `psdg_fn(...)` = PSDG total gradients.
**Measurement validity:** `cos(H1)` measures exactly the gradient compatibility between the PRO-framing and ABS-framing **total decision gradients**, aggregated as mean-of-gradients then cosine, in LoRA-parameter space.
**Unresolved uncertainty:** Only the *aggregate* cosine is persisted; per-sample gradients, their norms, and their decomposition are not.
**New experimental question:** What do per-sample `g_total` norms/distributions reveal that the aggregate cosine hides?

### Q3. Equivalent, partially overlapping, or fundamentally different?
**Answer: EQUIVALENT at the operational level.** The H1 construct (PRO/ABS total-gradient compatibility ≥ 0.80) and the `cos(H1)` measurement (cosine of PRO/ABS mean total gradients) coincide. The observable is *not* mismatched to the operational H1 claim.
**Caveat (partial-overlap nuance):** The scientific phrase 'subspace gradient compatibility' could be read more broadly (e.g., readout-vs-task alignment = H3). The protocol's *operational* H1 uses the PRO/ABS total-gradient cosine, so for H1 specifically construct equals measurement. The earlier 6E.20.6 tentative hidden-state / observable-mismatch framing is therefore **revised**: `cos(H1)` is a valid gradient-compatibility observable; the live question is its *sensitivity* and the *cause* of its ~0.45 plateau, not a construct/measurement mismatch.
**Unresolved uncertainty:** Sensitivity — can `cos(H1)` reach high values under ANY condition? (No constructed high-compatibility control exists in the frozen data.)
**New experimental question:** Include a sensitivity control to confirm `cos(H1)` can register compatibility when it is present.

### Q4. Four distinguishable causes of the ~0.45 plateau (each requires different telemetry)
**Observed evidence:** Frozen 6E.20.5: cos(H1) across all 7 conditions and all of steps {4,6,14,15} sits at ~0.41–0.46 (B0 0.4201, F1 0.4444, S2 0.4538, S4 0.4627, S8 0.4268, C4-Rand 0.4484, R1-Rand 0.4115). It is flat in step (r=+0.07) and shows no condition separation. Meanwhile `rho_c` carries the signal: S2 −0.0102, S4 +0.0058, S8 +0.0072, C4-Rand +0.0859 vs R1-Rand +0.0008; and d(15): F1 1.6176, R1-Rand 0.2535 (H5 supported). 6E.20.6: cos(H1) r≈0 with readout_b/margin/d/bal_acc; `rho_c` r=−0.45 with d, −0.65 with bal_acc (readout gradient opposed to the PSDG target exactly where behavior improves).

**Construct claim:** A single plateau value can arise from four distinct mechanisms; confounding them would misdirect any redesign.

**Measurement validity:** The current aggregate cos(H1) alone cannot distinguish the four causes. Resolving them requires persisting the per-sample and per-condition gradient primitives listed in section 2.

**Unresolved uncertainty:** Which of the four causes holds. Each implies a different follow-up:
- (a) **Genuine absence** of PRO/ABS total-gradient compatibility — the intervention does not align total gradients despite aligning subspaces.
- (b) **Background-dominated total gradients** — `g_total` is dominated by a large common signal across both framings, making the cosine of *total* gradients insensitive to a real but small *specific* (framing-unique) component. `||g_total||`, `||g_R||`, and the common-vs-specific decomposition (`g_total_common`, `g_total_specific`) are required to test this.
- (c) **Weak / ill-conditioned target** — the PSDG gradient direction is itself weak or noisy so the cosine is driven by the common background (related to b, but a property of the gradient target rather than its magnitude).
- (d) **Observable / intervention mismatch** — would be claimed only if a sensitivity control (Q5) can make cos(H1) high and it still does not (i.e., the observable cannot register compatibility at all). Per Q3, this is NOT the default reading; it must be disproven or proven by the control.

**New experimental question:** Run a sensitivity control (Q5); persist `||g_total||`, `||g_R||`, per-sample `rho_i`, per-sample `cos_H1`, and the cross-condition PRO/ABS decomposition to localize the cause (numeric classification in Section 3b).

### Q5. What would make cos(H1) scientifically invalid as THIS observable?
**Observed evidence:** A valid observable must (i) track its intended construct (it does — Q2/Q3) and (ii) be *sensitive* — able to move when the construct moves. No high-compatibility condition exists in the frozen data to demonstrate sensitivity.
**Construct claim:** Sensitivity is a precondition for using cos(H1) to *refute or support* H1 meaningfully.
**Pre-registered, non-degenerate SENS control (replaces any degenerate identity check):**
- *Construction:* SENS is a held-out probe set of N samples (N pre-fixed before any data) where PRO and ABS are **genuinely distinct** linguistic paraphrases of the **same** verdict (e.g., *The statement is true* vs *The statement is not false*). The two framings are distinct model inputs; no gradient vector is copied from one framing to the other.
- *Aggregation rule (pre-specified):* compute `g_total` for each framing through the **real frozen `psdg_fn`**; form `pos_mean`/`abs_mean` over SENS samples exactly as frozen `compute_raw_gradient_cosine`; `cos_H1(SENS) = cosine(pos_mean, abs_mean)`. This uses the frozen aggregation path — no new rule.
- *Expected compatibility criterion (pre-specified):* `cos_H1(SENS) >= 0.95`. If the model's naturally-computed gradients for paraphrase-same-verdict framings are not highly compatible, the observable cannot register compatibility that is present in the data.
- *Failure action (pre-specified, before observing data):* if `cos_H1(SENS) < 0.95` → the observable is declared **invalid for H1** (per Section 4); the broader premise stays unresolved; **no** H1/P1–P6 or protocol verdict is changed by SENS.
**Measurement validity — a priori falsification criteria (falsify the OBSERVABLE only, not the broader premise):**
1. SENS control `cos(H1)` < 0.95 → observable cannot register compatibility present in the data (the degenerate copy-gradient version is removed).
2. `cos(H1)` statistically indistinguishable (overlapping CIs, effect size < floor) across ALL conditions including S4/S8 vs C4-Rand/R1-Rand AND vs SENS → never tracks construct.
3. Aggregate algebraically forced constant (norms collapse / zero per-sample spread) → inert.
**Unresolved uncertainty:** All three checked at execution. **New experimental question:** Build and test SENS before redesign.

### Q6. Is 'underpowered' a defensible explanation from frozen evidence?
**Observed evidence:** 6E.20.5 used real LoRA autograd (Stage 6 completed genuine); cos(H1) flat across steps (r=+0.07); `rho_c` shows a clear, behavior-correlated signal (H5 supported); d(15) F1 1.6176 is a large effect. The H1 threshold is a protocol-fixed constant (0.80) frozen by P1–P6.
**Construct claim:** 'Underpowered' would mean the experiment could not detect a real effect due to insufficient precision/N. The frozen evidence shows a *different* pattern: a precise, reproducible ~0.45 plateau with a *separate* observable (`rho_c`) carrying the behavioral signal.
**Measurement validity:** Per the documented constraint, 'underpowered' must NOT be asserted unless frozen evidence supports it. It does not — the data are precise and the effect (for H5) is large; the cos(H1) result is a *plateau of a distinct observable*, not noise around a threshold.
**Unresolved uncertainty:** Whether the plateau is genuine-absence vs background-dominated (Q4 b/c) — both are design/mechanism questions, not power questions.
**New experimental question:** Do not relabel as underpowered; instead decompose g_total (Q4) to decide between (a) and (b/c).

### Q7. Reference standard for a valid observable (what a non-misleading observable must show)
**Observed evidence:** Reference observables in the same protocol — `rho_c` (moves, correlates r=−0.65 with bal_acc), `d` (F1 1.6176 vs R1-Rand 0.2535), `bal_acc`, `margin` — all vary with condition and carry interpretable structure.
**Construct claim:** A valid observable for the H1 construct should (i) vary when the construct varies, (ii) be separable from unrelated signals, (iii) not be dominated by an inert background in a way that hides the construct.
**Measurement validity:** cos(H1) currently satisfies (i) weakly (it varies little across all conditions) and fails to demonstrate (iii) without decomposition. This is the gap 6E.20.7 closes.
**Unresolved uncertainty:** Only the sensitivity control + decomposition can confirm (i)/(iii).
**New experimental question:** Adopt the Q5 sensitivity control and Q4 telemetry as the reference-standard gate for declaring cos(H1) valid.

## 3. A-priori observable-falsification criteria (summary)
1. Sensitivity control `cos(H1)` < 0.95 → observable cannot register compatibility.
2. `cos(H1)` indistinguishable across ALL conditions incl. control and S4/S8 → never tracks construct.

## 3b. Four-cause classification criteria (pre-registered, numeric)

All floors, denominators, and windows are fixed in this specification BEFORE any 6E.20.7 telemetry is observed. They are NOT tuned to data. The classification is diagnostic only and cannot alter H1/P1–P6 or any protocol verdict.

**Per condition c, checkpoint t (t in frozen {4,6,14,15}):**
- `C_common = ||g_total_common(c,t)||`
- `C_spec  = ||g_total_specific(c,t)||`
- `r_cs = C_spec / (C_common + eps)`, `eps = 1e-9` (relative specific-to-common ratio)
- `V_het(c,t) = std over samples of cos_H1_i` (per-sample heterogeneity)
- `agg(c,t) = cos_H1_aggregate` (frozen)

**Pre-registered floors (fixed before data):**
- `FLOOR_r_cs = 0.20` — below ⇒ specific component small relative to common.
- `FLOOR_Cspec_abs = 1e-3 * median(C_common over S4,S8)` — absolute negligibility of specific signal (the *formula* is fixed beforehand; only the median is computed at run time).
- `FLOOR_Vhet = 0.25` — above ⇒ heterogeneous.
- `FLOOR_Vhet_stable = 0.10` — below ⇒ tightly centered.
- `SENS_EXPECT = 0.95` — SENS control must meet (Q5).

**Comparison windows:** within-condition = both framings at same (c,t); across-condition = S4/S8 vs C4-Rand/R1-Rand at matched t; step-stability = `|agg range|` over {4,6,14,15} < 0.05.

**Four criteria (each evaluated independently on S4 and S8):**
1. *Common/background dominance:* `C_common >= median(C_common over conditions)` AND `r_cs < FLOOR_r_cs`, while `agg` stable (~0.45, |range|<0.05). ⇒ plateau pinned by shared background; `g_total_specific` exists but is masked.
2. *Low condition-specific gradient signal:* `C_spec < FLOOR_Cspec_abs` across S4/S8, independent of `C_common`. Distinguished from (1): (1) has non-negligible *absolute* `C_spec` but small *relative*; (2) has negligible *absolute* `C_spec`. If both relative and absolute conditions hold, classify (2) primary but record both flags.
3. *Per-sample heterogeneity:* `V_het > FLOOR_Vhet` OR multimodal `cos_H1_i` (pre-defined: >= 30% of samples in [+0.7,+1.0] AND >= 30% in [-1.0,+0.1]) ⇒ `agg` is a cancellation average.
4. *Genuine stable plateau:* `agg` stable (~0.45, |range|<0.05), `V_het < FLOOR_Vhet_stable`, `C_spec >= FLOOR_Cspec_abs` at a fixed angle (`|agg(S4)-agg(S8)|<0.05` and `|agg(S4)-agg(C4-Rand)|<0.10`), AND `cos_H1(SENS) >= SENS_EXPECT`. ⇒ 0.45 is a real stable value, not an artifact.

**Tie/overlap handling (global, pre-registered):** every criterion is evaluated independently and emits pass/fail + exceedance magnitude. When more than one is partially present, ALL triggered flags are reported with their numeric values; a primary classification is chosen ONLY by the largest exceedance ratio among triggered floors. No criterion is dropped; no post-hoc selection. The report lists every flag. This keeps the four explanations mutually interpretable under partial co-occurrence.

**Invariant:** `cos_H1_i` (per-sample) never enters `evaluate_protocol_hypotheses`; the `agg` used for any verdict is the frozen `cos_H1_aggregate`.

## 4. Decision rule for the observable question (pre-redesign)
- If Q5.1 passes (SENS `cos(H1)` ≥ 0.95) AND Q5.2/5.3 pass (it does move, not constant) → `cos(H1)` is a **valid observable**; proceed to mechanism decomposition (Q4) and the four-cause classification (Section 3b).
- If any of Q5.1–5.3 fail → `cos(H1)` is an **invalid observable** for H1; redesign the observable (not the broader premise) before any protocol change.
- Once the observable is declared valid, the four-cause classification (Section 3b) localizes the ~0.45 plateau. It is diagnostic only and cannot alter H1/P1–P6 or any protocol verdict.
